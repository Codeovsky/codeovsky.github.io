MdG Cold runs cold-storage operations. They needed a site that looked credible, loaded fast, and didn't fall over to basic security threats. We built all three.

### What we did

**Site rebuild from scratch.** Modern stack, sensible architecture, no plugin sprawl. Easy for the team to update, hard for things to silently break.

**Security hardening.** Proper headers, CSP rules, rate limiting on contact forms, regular checks. The boring stuff that keeps the site out of incident reports.

**Performance tuning.** Most cold-storage clients are checking the site from the road, on their phone, on patchy data. We optimized for that — not for a designer's laptop on fiber.

### The takeaway

Speed and security aren't features clients write home about. They're the baseline that lets everything else work.
