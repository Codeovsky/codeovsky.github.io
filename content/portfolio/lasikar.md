Lasikar is a suite of business tools — Lasikar OS, the SaaS app businesses run their day-to-day on; Lasikar AI, the intelligence layer on top; Lasikar Cloud, the infrastructure underneath; and the Lasikar mobile app that puts it all in your pocket. We've worked with the team across all of it.

### What we did

**Brought AI into the product properly.** Not "we have AI now" sprinkled on a feature flag — actual workflow improvements where the AI does something useful and obvious. The kind of thing where users notice it works, not the kind where you have to point it out.

**Built the mobile app.** The same Lasikar workflows, redesigned for phone-first use. Field staff and on-the-go owners can do most of what the web app does without opening a laptop. Same backend, same data, native feel.

**Cut the cloud bill, kept the performance.** Same throughput, same response times, significantly less infrastructure spend. Most fast-growing SaaS bills have at least 30–50% slack in them once you actually look.

**Produced the brand animation work.** Scripted, character-driven pieces that explain Lasikar without sounding like a generic SaaS explainer video. Every business has a personality — these animations carry Lasikar's.

### The takeaway

Lasikar's a long-running relationship — not a one-time gig. Web, AI, mobile, cloud, animations, all under one roof, all moving in the same direction. That's the kind of work we enjoy most.
