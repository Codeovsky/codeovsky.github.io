## What's in the name

Codeovsky is two pieces stitched together — *code* and *ovsky*.

The **code** part is the obvious one. We love building things. Math, logic, careful systems that hold up under pressure. The day-to-day craft of writing software that actually works.

The **ovsky** is a quiet nod to Andrei Tarkovsky, the Russian filmmaker. He once described cinema as "a mosaic made with time" — small fragments arranged carefully until the whole thing means more than any of its pieces. That's how we like to work too. Not just code in isolation, but code stitched to design, design stitched to strategy, strategy stitched to the actual people who'll use the thing. Ideas pulled from computer science, philosophy, finance, psychology — wherever the problem actually lives.

We don't think of ourselves as just a "tech company." We think of the work as building little worlds where things can grow. Sometimes that's a SaaS platform. Sometimes it's a brand identity. Sometimes it's a marketing campaign. The medium changes; the care doesn't.

## Who we are

We started small — a few friends from different backgrounds working on each other's problems. Communication design, computer science, data science, business, finance. We still operate that way. There's no big sales team in front of you, no account managers running interference. You talk to the people doing the work. If we're building your app, the person on the call wrote the code. If we're running your campaigns, the person on the call drafted the post.

That setup matters because **most of our clients are small and medium businesses** — the kind of work where decisions move fast and the wrong agency layer slows everything down. We're built to move with you.

## How we actually work

Honest, direct conversations. Plain English instead of jargon. We tell you what we think, even when it's not what you want to hear — and we expect the same back. The relationship works better that way.

We treat your business like our own. That's not a slogan; it's just self-interest. Our reputation lives or dies on whether your business actually grows after working with us. So we don't take projects we don't believe in, and we don't disappear after delivery.

When we start with someone new, we usually begin with a real look at the business. Not a 50-slide audit deck — just honest conversations about what's working, what isn't, where the money comes from, where it leaks out. From there we figure out what would actually move the needle, and we build accordingly.

Sometimes that's a new web app. Sometimes it's a cleaner cloud setup that cuts your monthly bill in half. Sometimes it's a brand refresh and a Q3 ad campaign. Often it's all of the above, layered in over months, because real businesses are messy and the easy fix usually isn't the right one.

## What we believe

We don't think technology alone fixes anything. Tech is a tool — a powerful one, but still just a tool. What actually matters is whether the tool helps real people do real things better than they did yesterday. That belief shapes how we pick projects, how we price, and how we say no to work that's just noise.

We also care about the people doing the work — including ourselves. We're not chasing scale for its own sake. We're trying to do good work, with people we like, for clients we believe in. That sounds quaint until you've worked with the alternative.
