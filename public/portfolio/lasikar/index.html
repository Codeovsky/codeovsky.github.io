SaaS AI Cloud Animation

LASIKAR

Lasikar is a suite of business tools — Lasikar OS (a SaaS app businesses run their day-to-day on), Lasikar AI, and Lasikar Cloud. We’ve worked with the team across all three.

Two big wins worth mentioning. First, we helped bring AI into the product in a way that actually felt useful instead of bolted-on for the sake of saying “we have AI.” Second, and we’re particularly proud of this one, we cut their cloud bill significantly — same performance, much cheaper infrastructure.

We also produced the brand animations for Lasikar. These were a fun project — short, scripted, character-driven pieces that explain what Lasikar does without sounding like a generic SaaS explainer. Every business has its own personality, and the animations reflect that.

What we did: AI integration, cloud cost optimization, brand animation, motion design.
